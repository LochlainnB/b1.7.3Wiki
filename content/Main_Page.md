Welcome to the **Minecraft Beta 1.7.3 Wiki**, a reference for the version
released on 8 July 2011 and still the most-played build of the Beta era.

Everything here describes the game *as it behaves in Beta 1.7.3*. There are no
version histories and no notes about later releases: if a page says a block has
a hardness of 2, that is what the 1.7.3 jar does.

## Start here

- [[Items]] — tools, food, materials and everything else in the inventory
- [[Mobs]] — the creatures that spawn in the world
- [[Mining]] — how blocks break, what tools change, and what drops
- [[Crafting]] — how the crafting grid works, and every recipe
- [[Smelting]] — the furnace, fuels and smelting recipes

## Reference

The wiki's data comes straight out of the game. Block IDs, hardness values,
sprites and recipes are read from the Beta 1.7.3 client jar rather than typed in
by hand, so they are correct by construction.
