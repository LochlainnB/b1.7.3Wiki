**Block of Diamond** is a block crafted from nine [[Diamond|diamonds]].

## Obtaining

### Breaking

A block of diamond drops itself when [[Mining#Harvest levels|mined]] with an
iron or diamond pickaxe. Mined with anything else, it drops nothing.
