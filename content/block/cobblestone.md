**Cobblestone** is the block dropped when [[Stone]] is mined with a pickaxe. It
is one of the most abundant building materials in the game and the primary
ingredient of stone tools and several utility blocks.

## Obtaining

[[Mining]] any [[Stone]] block with a [[Wooden Pickaxe|pickaxe]] drops one
cobblestone. Mining cobblestone itself also drops cobblestone, so it can be
moved freely once placed. Breaking it by hand or with any other tool destroys
the block without a drop.

Cobblestone also generates naturally as part of [[Dungeon]] floors and, in the
world, wherever flowing [[Lava]] meets [[Water]].
