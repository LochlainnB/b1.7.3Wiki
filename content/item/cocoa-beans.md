**Cocoa beans** are the brown [[Dye|dye]] found in [[Dungeon|dungeon]] chests.

## Obtaining

### Dungeon chests

A [[Dungeon#Chest loot|dungeon chest]] draw gives cocoa beans 1 time in 11.
Nothing else gives cocoa beans.
