This is a reference for **Minecraft Beta 1.7.3**, released on 8 July 2011.

## Scope

The wiki documents the game *as Beta 1.7.3 behaves*. It is written for people
playing and modding that version today, so:

- There are **no version history sections**. A page describes how something
  works now, in 1.7.3, and nothing else.
- There is **no edition split**. Beta 1.7.3 is a single Java release.
- Statements about behaviour should be checkable against the 1.7.3 client or
  server, not against a later version or a memory of one.

## Where the data comes from

Block and item IDs, hardness, blast resistance, light levels, sprites, biome
colours and every crafting and smelting recipe are extracted directly from the
Beta 1.7.3 client jar by the scripts in `tools/extract/`. They are not typed in
by hand.

That means infoboxes, recipe grids and the [[Data values]] tables are correct by
construction, and a page never has to restate a number the game already knows.
Prose is the part humans and language models write.
